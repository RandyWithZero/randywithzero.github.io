AI Video Studio For Future Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 1, 2026

Home Terms of Service

1. Overview

AI Video Studio For Future is an AI-powered content creation and publishing platform for short-form videos. This Privacy Policy explains how AI Video Studio collects, uses, stores, and protects information when users access our website or authorize publishing features.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information that users provide directly, such as account information, contact email, uploaded media, captions, titles, descriptions, and publishing preferences.

When users connect a social media account, AI Video Studio may receive authorization tokens and limited account information made available through the user's OAuth authorization.

3. OAuth Permissions and Platform Access

AI Video Studio For Future uses OAuth authorization to request permission from users before accessing supported platform features. For content publishing workflows, the application may request permissions needed to upload video content as drafts or publish videos when the user chooses to do so.

AI Video Studio For Future does not access a user's account without authorization and does not publish content unless the user initiates the action.

4. How We Use Information

We use information to authenticate users, process video content, prepare captions and metadata, upload or publish user-selected content, provide support, improve service reliability, and comply with legal or platform requirements.

5. Data Sharing

AI Video Studio For Future does not sell, rent, or trade personal information. We may share information only with service providers or platform APIs as necessary to provide the requested functionality, comply with law, prevent abuse, or protect the security of the service.

6. Data Storage and Retention

We retain information only for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, or support user-requested publishing workflows. Users may request deletion of their data by contacting us.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user data, including secure handling of OAuth tokens and limiting access to information required for service operation.

8. User Choices

Users can revoke platform authorization through the relevant social media account settings. Users may also contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of their information.

9. Children's Privacy

AI Video Studio For Future is not intended for children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
